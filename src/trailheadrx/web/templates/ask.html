Figuring out your insurance plan's rules for a medication is hard.

Trailhead Rx reads your plan's actual policy and shows you what it takes to get the medication your doctor has in mind.

What this does

Your plan may require you to try other medications before you can get the one you want. Trailhead Rx outlines the steps your doctor needs to follow, how long it usually takes, and how to appeal if you get turned down.

It also shows the routes around the plan — other ways to get your medication, including free first fills, savings cards, buying direct from the manufacturer, and assistance programs — with what each one costs and what it gives up.

Right now it covers Ohio plans and migraine medicines. The rules it reads are the real ones the plans publish, and every statement is tied to the page it came from. It is not medical advice and does not decide your case.
